## Scope

Primary target: `src/app/[locale]/page.tsx`. Mode: Persuade. Audience: Customer Success managers and CS leaders evaluating whether Waslix can turn portfolio evidence into accountable work without opaque AI claims. Primary action: explore the product workflow; secondary action: start a workspace.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Waslix opens with a physical customer-success operating system, then becomes a precise product story. The page refuses both the category-default glossy SaaS hero and decorative network spectacle: one engineered portfolio moves from customer visibility to explainable health to accountable action across exactly three sections.

OWN-WORLD: The opening is a near-black precision field containing graphite customer modules, thin evidence traces and a layered circular Waslix hub with restrained teal tracks. Compact product cards reuse the dashboard's rules, radii, typography and semantic health colors; glow, glass, rainbow light and particle spectacle do not. Below the third section, the established graphite product UI resumes unchanged.

STORY: The visitor sees Waslix connect a customer portfolio, follows one illustrative account as usage, support, meeting, task, note and CRM evidence forms an explainable health state, then watches risk become analysis, recommendation, owned work and renewal preparation. The same world persists throughout before handing attention to the real product interfaces below.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact product-aligned navigation sits above an asymmetric split. The claim and actions occupy the inline-start side while a wide customer ecosystem fills the inline end: six small account modules converge on a modest layered Waslix hub, with three quiet dashboard-status cards providing recognizable product evidence. The scene has no frame or visible canvas boundary and never competes with the headline.

FORM: The user-pinned Waslix customer-success system replaces the abstract Signal Core; seed key `waslix-system-2026`. A sticky canvas exists only inside the three-section opening wrapper. ScrollTrigger controls portfolio, customer-health and action-workflow states; pointer response stays below four degrees, reduced motion holds a static network, and adaptive quality removes secondary structure before reducing resolution.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
