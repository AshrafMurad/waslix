## Scope

Primary target: `src/app/[locale]/page.tsx`. Mode: Persuade. Audience: Customer Success managers and CS leaders evaluating whether Waslix can turn portfolio evidence into accountable work without opaque AI claims. Primary action: explore the product workflow; secondary action: start a workspace.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Waslix is a live operations rail where customer evidence visibly advances into owned work. The page refuses the category-default glossy SaaS hero and disconnected feature-card grid; every prominent object shows source evidence, health impact, owner, due window, or action state in the same visual language as the product.

OWN-WORLD: The visual world extends the Waslix dashboard: graphite background, white and raised-neutral surfaces, teal identity and interaction, and green, amber, and red reserved for semantic state. Geometry stays at the product's 6-8px radius, borders stay precise, and controls feel compact, labelled, stateful, and enterprise-ready. Three-dimensional motion is limited to evidence moving along a rail.

STORY: A visitor first operates an illustrative portfolio rather than watching a decorative mockup. They follow evidence from a changed account through explainable health, ownership, and a next action, then see the same chain carry through account context, lifecycle, renewals, and team operations. The close turns product understanding into workspace creation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact product-aligned navigation sits above an asymmetric split. The claim and product-exploration action occupy the start side; a large working portfolio rail occupies the end side and crosses the fold. A bounded Three.js field carries teal evidence points behind the rail while the foreground UI remains crisp, semantic, and immediately interactive.

FORM: Built from grounded candidate 7, the selected Live operations rail composition, seed key `ace530f8`. The rail preserves one continuous evidence path across otherwise varied sections; interaction changes the active account while keeping source, score, owner, and action visible. The composition uses scale and motion for product clarity, never visual spectacle detached from the workflow.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
