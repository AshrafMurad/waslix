# 02 · Positioning and vision

## Positioning statement

For growing B2B SaaS and service teams managing customer relationships with scattered tools, Waslix is a Customer Success Workspace that combines essential lifecycle work with explainable customer intelligence and clear next actions.

**Category:** Customer Success Workspace.  
**Audience:** Small and medium B2B SaaS and service companies.  
**Promise:** Know who needs attention. Understand why. Take action.

## Four differentiators

| Differentiator                    | Product proof                                                     |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Attention-first customer success  | One prioritized account row groups relevant concerns              |
| Explainable customer intelligence | Scores, source freshness, trends and reasons appear together      |
| Insight-to-action workflows       | Recommendations become tasks or playbooks with explicit ownership |
| Useful from day one               | Sensible defaults, manual inputs and CSV import                   |

These are product intentions, not verified competitive superiority claims.

## Product boundary

Waslix owns customer-success context: health, risks, goals, onboarding, renewal preparation, follow-ups and history. External CRM, support, billing and usage systems can provide context later. Waslix does not implement their full operational workflows.

SaaS and services share the adoption dimension; raw usage semantics may differ. Avoid forcing every customer to have seats, logins or subscription billing.

## Brand

Professional, modern, calm, clear, intelligent and human. The name's connection theme supports the relationship story without requiring repeated explanation of its origin.

Approved message:

> Waslix is a customer success workspace for growing B2B teams that brings customer health, onboarding, risks, renewals and daily follow-ups into one focused place.

Short line:

> Know who needs attention. Understand why. Take action.

Avoid presenting V1 as autonomous AI, an all-in-one enterprise suite, predictive churn software or a replacement for a CRM.

## Growth path

After V1 evidence supports expansion: one real usage or CRM integration, then stronger integrations, configurable health, segmentation, custom playbooks/automation and deeper analytics. AI-assisted summaries or renewal briefs may follow; deterministic facts and permissions remain authoritative.

This is a direction, not a release commitment. Do not introduce future infrastructure simply because an item appears here.

## Portfolio narrative

The project should demonstrate coherent product design, relational domain modeling, workspace isolation, authorization, background processing, explainable rules, meaningful tests and disciplined enterprise UI. Feature count and technology count are not success measures.
